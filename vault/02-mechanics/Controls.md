# Controls

## The two schemes

| Scheme | Specials | Damage | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kata ("form") | Six buttons and motion inputs: ↓↘→ (slot A), →↓↘ (B), ↓↙← (C), ↓↓ (D) | 100%, plus a 10% precision bonus on motion-input specials | Players who want execution |
| Kihon ("basics") | Six buttons plus a Special button: neutral (A), forward (B), back (C), down (D) | 100% | Newcomers |

## Rules

- One-button specials never outperform manual inputs (P4).
- Combos and cancels work the same in both schemes (K3).
- Kata is the first cut at the week-1 scope gate if the schedule slips. Kihon is never cut (K4).
- The control-select screen explains the precision bonus in one line.
