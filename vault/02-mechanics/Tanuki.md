# The Tanuki

## How the boss fights

The Tanuki fights with Ryo's normals and starters plus one copied special (T0). When shapeshifted it uses Ryo's model and clips, so copying needs no new animation. It reacts in 15 frames, the fastest in the game. The copy rule is never cut.

## The copy rule

| # | Rule | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Lock point | The copy is chosen when Ryo enters the Tanuki's node |
| T2 | Target | Ryo's highest-level special in a slot, throws included. Sold and replaced moves don't count |
| T3 | Ties | The one levelled most recently, then the lower slot |
| T4 | Strength | At Ryo's level, but never his modifiers or evolution |
| T5 | Always defined | The merchant won't release Ryo's last special |
| T6 | Visible all run | The target's talisman glows in the HUD, and the rest-stop forecast names it |

## The move, not the bond

The Tanuki steals the move, not the bond behind it, so an evolved Lv 3 move always beats its copy (T4). The move the player invested in most is the one they must out-think. In the GDD's walkthrough the Tanuki throws plain Foxfire and the player's Piercing Foxfire passes straight through it.

A line that has the Tanuki take, use or boast about an evolution or a modifier breaks the copy rule.

## The forecast

At each rest stop a forecast shows the Tanuki's copied move, its level and its expected cost from the player's own fights, so the risk is visible before choosing to heal, shop or train.

## Counterplay

| What the Tanuki does | How the player answers |
|---|---|
| Copies the highest-level special | Evolve it to Lv 3: the copy never gets the evolution |
| Copies the special without its modifier | Attach a modifier that wins the mirror, such as a faster projectile |
| Copies only one special | Keep a second special that beats the first |

## Open questions

- Ticket YOK-5 lists four Tanuki moves: Leaf Shift, Belly Drum, Teakettle Guard and Illusion Rush, each with counterplay, and says the boss "mixes every archetype's tricks". None of them is in the extended GDD, which gives the Tanuki Ryo's normals, his starters and one copied special, with no new animation. They are left out of this note until the designer says which is right.
