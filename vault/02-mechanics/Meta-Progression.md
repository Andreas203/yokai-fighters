# Meta-progression

## Unlocks by depth

Twelve of the 33 abilities start locked: 3 specials, 8 modifiers and 1 cancel rule. Evolutions are never locked.

| Run reaches | Row 5 or earlier | Rows 6–8 | The Tanuki | Tanuki win |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unlocks | 0 | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| Best special carried at Lv 2 | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

- Unlocks are awarded at the end of a run, in a fixed order, so pushing on always beats dying on purpose (M1).
- Twelve locked abilities take roughly 4–6 runs (M2).

## The carry-over

The best special is carried into the next run at Lv 2 if the run reached row 6 or further (M3). It is the one exception to new moves starting at Lv 1. In the fiction, one talisman stays faintly inked.

## Story save

Story progress is saved separately, so the first reveal and the first-loss card never depend on unlocks (M4).

## Scope gate

Scope-gate cut candidate: the meta-unlock layer is the second cut at the end of week 1, after Kata. The carry-over and the story stay even if unlocks are cut.

## Open questions

- The fixed unlock order of the 12 locked abilities is not written yet.
