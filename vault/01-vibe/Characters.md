# Characters

## Ryo

The player character, an apprentice exorcist. Earnest and stubborn. His master taught him that binding borrows a spirit's strength and a debt must be named, so he apologises to every yokai he binds. He fights with six normals and two starter specials, Spirit Wave and Rising Talisman, and his own hit sparks are ink black.

Folklore reference: ofuda, the paper talismans of shrine and exorcist practice. Reference images: `docs/design/characters/ryo/`.

## Master Sōen

Ryo's teacher, seen only in the intro card. Patient and wry. Her rule, "name the debt", is the line the ending pays off.

Reference images: none yet.

## The merchant and the Tanuki

The rest-stop trader and the final boss are the same creature. Cheerful, generous and quietly hungry. It collects techniques the way others collect teacups, and helps Ryo grow so there is more to steal. It steals the move, not the bond behind it. Its stage is a tea house whose paper screens flicker between seasons.

Folklore reference: tanuki are shapeshifting tricksters. In the tale Bunbuku Chagama a tanuki turns itself into a teakettle; other tales have them pay with leaves disguised as money and drum on their bellies.

Reference images: none yet. When shapeshifted for the fight, the Tanuki uses Ryo's model.

## Kitsune

The fox of the bamboo grove at dusk, a zoner who holds the screen with slow foxfire. She teases and slips away. She wears a full white fox mask with red markings for the whole fight, and has three tails; the Nine-Tailed elder has nine. Her hit sparks are foxfire orange.

Folklore reference: kitsune are fox spirits that grow more tails with age, up to nine, and kitsunebi (foxfire) is the ghost light they carry.

Reference images: `docs/design/characters/kitsune/`.

## Oni

The rushdown yokai of the snowbound mountain shrine gate. Proud and blunt, it dashes in and swings an iron club. Its hit sparks are oni red.

Folklore reference: oni carry the kanabō, an iron club; the saying "an oni with an iron club" means strength made stronger.

Reference images: none yet.

## Kappa

The grappler of the lantern riverbank. Formal, stubborn and polite, it walks straight into throw range. Its hit sparks are kappa teal.

Folklore reference: kappa are river spirits that love sumo and cucumbers and carry water in a dish on their heads; bow to one and it bows back, spilling the water.

Reference images: none yet.

## The yokai as a group

The yokai are real spirits trapped in the Tanuki's illusion and made to fight again each loop, which is why Ryo's apologies matter. They are opponents and sources of power, never villains. Only three yokai are in the build: Kitsune, Oni and Kappa, each with an elder.

## Not in the 5-week build

The GDD lists stretch goals that must not appear in shipped content: the yokai Jorōgumo and Tengu, the characters Tetsu and Kaede, shrines, supers, Acts 2 and 3, and the Yūrei gauntlet.
