
Everything is drawn in flat cel colour of 2-3 tone steps per surface, simple large shapes, a low level of detail. All surfaces are matte with soft even light and no cast shadows. There are NO lanterns, lamps, lights, fires, candles or glowing objects of any kind, and no light sources in the scene. No people, yokai, animals or creatures, no buildings, bridges, shrines or boats, no modern objects and no text of any kind. The palette is limited to indigo, ink, pine, water teal, brown, moss green and rice paper; no bright blues, no turquoise, no neon, no pure white. The mood is still, damp, murky and a little eerie, but quiet, never horror. Avoid: photorealism, PBR detail, metallic sheen, painterly brush noise, film grain, text, watermark, logo, signature, glowing particles, fireflies, will-o'-wisps, cast shadows, busy background, any resemblance to an existing franchise, artist or character.
