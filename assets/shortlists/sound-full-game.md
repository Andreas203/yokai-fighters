# Sound shortlist: full game, $20 cap (YOK-54, extends sound-demo-slice.md)

Supersedes the demo shortlist for planning. Sound is sourced (AM1). The designer decides and buys; nothing here is purchased. The designer set a **$20 total cap** on sound spend. The earlier $93.97 and $138.97 sets are kept below under "Beyond cap".

Prices and licences re-checked 2026-10-15 from the store pages. itch.io prices are "or more" minimums. **Licence caveat:** no page names student capstone or public showcase use. CC0 needs no permission for either. "Royalty-free" and CC BY cover both in practice; packs marked (unverified) should get written confirmation from the seller.
