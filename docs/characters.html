Player character · apprentice exorcist

Ryoリョウ

  • earnest
  • stubborn
  • ink-black sparks

Ryo is an apprentice under Master Sōen. He is earnest and stubborn, speaks rarely, and never jokes at a yokai’s expense. He binds spirits into paper talismans, apologises to each one, and has promised to give every one of them back.

He fights with six normal attacks and two starter specials, Spirit Wave and Rising Talisman. Four ofuda wrap his right forearm, one for each special slot. His hit sparks are ink black.

Design: a high collar pulled up to the nose, so his eyes carry the expression. An indigo coat with brass spiral buttons, a rope sash with an ofuda pouch, shin wraps and split-toe tabi. A vermilion binding mark sits under his left eye.

Rest-stop trader · final boss

The Merchant and the Tanuki

  • cheerful
  • generous
  • quietly hungry

One creature in two coats. The merchant is warm, helpful and a little too interested in Ryo’s best move. The Tanuki is what he is when the disguise drops. It collects techniques the way others collect teacups, and helps Ryo grow so there is more to steal.

It steals the move, not the bond behind it, so an evolved Level 3 move always beats its copy. In the fight it takes Ryo’s shape and uses his model, plus one copied special. It reacts faster than any other opponent in the game. Its stage is a tea house whose paper screens flicker between seasons.

FolkloreTanuki are shapeshifting tricksters. In the tale Bunbuku Chagama one turns itself into a teakettle, and others pay with leaves disguised as money.

Yokai · the zoner · bamboo grove at dusk

Kitsune狐

  • teasing
  • slips away
  • foxfire orange

The fox of the bamboo grove holds the screen with slow foxfire. She teases and slips away. A full white fox mask with red markings hides her face for the whole fight, and she has three tails.

Her temperaments: aggressive, she throws foxfire at mid range while advancing. Patient, she holds the far side of the screen and anti-airs jumps. Her one habit to punish: she often jumps after a knockdown.

Her elder, the Nine-Tailed Kitsune, reflects any special used twice in a row. It teaches players to mix their specials, and offers the cancel rule Fox Step.

FolkloreKitsune are fox spirits that gain tails with age, up to nine. Kitsunebi, foxfire, is the ghost light they carry.

Yokai · the rushdown · snowbound shrine gate

Oni

  • proud
  • blunt
  • oni red

The Oni dashes in and swings an iron club. Its relentless pressure teaches the player to block. It offers Iron-Club Charge, Oni Quake and the Oni modifiers.

Its elder has armour on every special.

FolkloreOni carry the kanabō, an iron club. “An oni with an iron club” means strength made stronger.

The yokai are not villains

The yokai are real spirits trapped in the Tanuki’s illusion and made to fight again each loop, which is why Ryo’s apologies matter. They are opponents and sources of power, never villains. Three yokai are in this build: Kitsune, Oni and Kappa, each with an elder.

The Oni and Kappa personalities above are proposals from the design notes, and their concept art has not been made yet.
