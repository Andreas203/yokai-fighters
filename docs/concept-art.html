The look

A muted ukiyo-e palette

Toon-shaded 3D with ink outlines. The palette is persimmon, indigo, rice paper and pine. The interface is made of paper talismans, brush strokes and red seals.

Indigo#2D3A5E
Ink#1D1B21
Persimmon#D8632C
Rice paper#F1E8D4
Pine#34483B
Foxfire#F2A23A
Shrine red#B5332B

Hit sparks carry the source’s colour: ink black for Ryo, foxfire orange for the Kitsune, red for the Oni, teal for the Kappa.
