Character sketch · first pass · player character

Ryoリョウ

Apprentice exorcist under Master Sōen. He binds yokai into ofuda and fights with what they lend him, and he has promised to give every one of them back.

退魔

Description

Silhouette

Lean and upright, with a wide stance. Black undercut hair with one lock over his right eye. A high collar zipped to the nose, so his eyes carry every expression.

Outfit

Indigo coat with spiral brass buttons, closed left over right like a kimono, its tail cut long on one side. Shimenawa rope sash with shide paper, indigo trousers, kyahan shin wraps and jika-tabi.

Power

Ink-black energy with brush edges, matching his hit sparks (V5). Four ofuda wrap his forearm, one per special slot (A1). Two carry red seals, for Spirit Wave and Rising Talisman. His ready pose holds an ofuda up between two fingers.

Palette

Indigocoat, collar, trousers
Inkhair, energy, tabi

"Forgive me. I'll set your spirit free."

Head

The face is two eyes and a mark. The vermilion stroke under his left eye is the binding mark, one stroke per yokai he holds.

Silhouette test

Still readable as solid black: raised talisman arm, wide stance, one-sided coat tail.

Spirit Wave

13f startup · 30f recovery · projectile

Thrown ofuda breaks into a Hokusai curl. At Lv 3 it becomes Great Wave and absorbs one projectile.

Rising Talisman

5f startup · 8f active · 28f recovery · invulnerable f1–5

A talisman strip spirals up around him as an anti-air. At Lv 3 it becomes Heaven Seal and launches.

For the designer to decide

  1. Collar over the mouth. Story cards would show only eyes and brows. Keep it closed, or open the collar for card art and the Win 3 ending?
  2. Wraps as slot readout. Lighting the forearm wraps per filled slot puts loadout on the model. In the Tanuki fight, the copied move's wrap could glow to match the HUD talisman (T6). Is that worth the extra material work?
  3. Coat tail. It needs extra bones on the Meshy rig with sway driven from the tick count, or a rigid bake, so the 60-tick loop stays deterministic. Asset Smith records the choice in the rig plan.
  4. Name kanji. Katakana for now. 涼 (cool) fits his calm. 了 (to finish, to settle) fits "I'll set your spirit free."
