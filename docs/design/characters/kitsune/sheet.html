Kitsuneキツネ

The fox of the bamboo grove at dusk. A zoner who holds the screen with slow foxfire, teases you into jumping, then steps through her own illusion to hit you from behind.

狐

Description

Silhouette

Fox ears, white hair tipped orange, and three tails fanned out behind her. Her outline says fox before you look at her face.

Outfit

Cropped white haori with wide sleeves and red trim over an indigo top. Foxfire obi with a suzu bell, shrine-red hakama, black tabi and geta. A full white fox mask hides her face all fight; only her foxfire eyes show.

Power

She is the zoner: Foxfire at mid range while she advances, and she anti-airs your jumps (Y2). She holds foxfire palm-up and casts with a fox hand sign. Her orange matches the foxfire hit spark (V5). Her stage is the bamboo grove at dusk (V9).

Head

The mask is her face. Two foxfire eyes in the slits are all you see, and they flare on Foxfire startup.

Silhouette test

Solid black, she still reads as a fox: ears, three tails and the wide sleeves. Ryo's shape is upright and narrow, so the two never blur.

Foxfire

15f startup · 4f active · 30f recovery · slow projectile

A drifting flame that leaves fading copies behind it. At Lv 3 it becomes Piercing Foxfire and passes through projectiles.

Fox Mirage

18f startup · 10f recovery · teleport behind

She dissolves into her mask and reappears behind you. At Lv 3 it becomes Mirage Feint and leaves a decoy that absorbs one hit.

For the designer to decide

  1. Tail count. Three for the common Kitsune and nine for the Nine-Tailed elder? Or one tail count for both, with the elder told apart by colour and VFX only, so the variant stays a level preset?
  2. Tails and sleeves in motion. Neither may use hand-keyed animation. They need extra bones with sway on the Meshy rig, or a procedural sway driven from the tick count so it stays deterministic. Check both against the YOK-29 model shortlist.
  3. Temperament in the pose. This is the patient stance: orb out, hand sign up. Should the aggressive profile get its own idle, such as leaning forward with the tails flared, or share this one?
