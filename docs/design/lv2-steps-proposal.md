# Lv 2 steps for every special (YOK-36)

Designer decision 2026-10-20 (A4, E21): Movesmith proposes one Lv 2 step per special that lacks one; rules-lawyer checks; the designer approves the list. Everything marked `proposed` is open until then. Each step must change how you fight (P1), stay inside the clip's truth (F2: a retime of 0.5x-2x, holds of 4 frames or fewer, or move data), and differ from the Lv 3 evolution (A5). Lv 3 keeps the Lv 2 step (E17).

Where move data exists, the step is in the move file's `levels.2` block. Where it does not (no move file yet), the step lives only here and the move's author takes it when the file is written.

## Notes for the rules-lawyer and designer

- Every step is a data change (A5-style: no new animation, F2 clip truth kept). Frame numbers in the "frames" field of a card move with the clip-derived Lv 1 when it lands.
- No damage-only steps (E21, P1). No new specials, modifiers or cancel rules (A8).
- Slippery Skin (throw-invulnerable during the carrying move's startup) conflicts with none of these: River Grab's step is on recovery.
- A copied special (T0) takes whatever the Tanuki's most-invested level is; every step above works with Ryo's clips (T, C4 copy safety). Fox Mirage, Iron-Club Charge, Oni Quake, River Grab and Shell Spin are not Ryo's own, so their copy case is for the Tanuki to use Ryo's casting clips with the same data; data is the same in Kata and Kihon (K3).
- Open for the designer: whether the Oni Quake clip delta (27 / 4 / 25 against the table 22 / 5 / 26) stays; whether Iron-Club Charge's 30% is the right size (suggest a trial against the dummy at one stage width before it is final).
